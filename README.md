One joke about Monday mornings: Monday comes collecting what the weekend took. (114 characters)

## The line

> Monday called: it wants its snooze button back, your motivation, and the weekend you swore you'd use productively.

## Why it works

Monday is framed as a debt collector calling in three things at once — the snooze button, your motivation, and the weekend itself. The last item is the punchline: the weekend you swore you'd use productively is already gone, and Monday knows it.
